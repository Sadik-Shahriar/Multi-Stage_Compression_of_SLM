## **Good Research Questions Are…** 

- Concise 

   - Timely 

- Clear 

   - Theoretically rich 

- Operationalizable 

   - Bold 

- Open-ended 

- Elegant 

- Intellectually challenging 

## **No Consensus on “Good”** 

# ● There is no agreement on what makes a good research question! 

# Some (Main) Question Types to Avoid 

…at least without other supporting questions 

## **Naïve or Very Simple Questions** 

- Obvious questions 

# “ – _Does mobile learning support anytime, anywhere learning?_ ” 

- Low-level questions 

   - “ 

   - – _What is the average bandwidth among_ 

   - _Swedish Internet users?_ ” 

- Yes/No questions 

   - “ 

   - – _Do students like using the YouLearn_ 

   - _system?_ ” 

## **Value-Laden Questions** 

- Hidden premises 

   - “ 

   - – _Why do Facebook users prefer virtual_ 

   - _contacts over face-to-face contacts?_ ” 

- Biased questions 

   - “ 

   - – _Why are schoolteachers reluctant to_ 

   - _adopt IT in their classes?_ ” 

- Preaching questions 

“ – _What can be done to increase the use of Internet for self-development instead of entertainment?_ ” 

## **Attribution Problem** 

- Was it our action that made the change? 

“ ● _To what extent does IT awareness training in the Ministry of Education help to improve learning outcomes in schools?_ ”

## Stockholms universitet **Measuring Non-Measurable Things** 

- Some subjects do not have easily measurable indicators 

“ – _What is the aesthetic difference between computer-generated music and Mozart’s music?_ ” 

## **Unfalsifiable Questions** 

- “Can X be used…” and “How can X be used…” 

“ – _Can one-to-one computing be used to improve learning outcomes in Swedish language courses?_ ” 

- Looking for a problem 

   - “ 

   - – _What can we do with mobile phones in_ 

   - _developing countries?_ ” 

## **Ambiguous Questions** 

- Unclear questions 

“ – _What does e-government change?_ ” 

“ – _Where does e-business apply?_ ” 

- Multiple questions in one 

“ – _What would students like to improve with YouLearn or do they prefer other learning systems?_ ” 

## **Uninteresting Questions** 

- “Well, so what?” questions 

“ – _What kinds of m-government solutions would have helped the 2010 elections?_ ”
