## **Course Objective** 

This course introduces students to the exciting area of “Green Computing" aiming to help students acquire the knowledge and skills needed to do research in this space, as it is critical to managing consumption of societal energy usage more efficiently for long-term sustainability.  This course is organizationally divided into two tracks. The first track is “Energy-Efficient Computing”, and the second track is “Applying Computing towards Sustainability”.

## **Midterm Assessment** 

Page **5** of **12** 

The Midterm Project addresses an open-ended **Complex Engineering Problem (EP)** with multiple acceptable solutions. Students will **design, analyze, and justify a computing-based sustainability solution** using one or more of the following approaches: **IoT systems, data analytics, or machinelearning techniques** . 

The project may involve **monitoring, modeling, prediction, optimization, or decision support** related to sustainability challenges such as energy usage, carbon-aware computing, environmental quality, or resource efficiency in computing-intensive environments. 

Students are required to **identify a relevant sustainability problem** , clearly state assumptions, select appropriate data sources or sensing mechanisms, and develop a **computational artifact** (e.g., dashboard, analytical model, visualization, or prototype system) that provides both **detailed and summarized insights** . 

The solution must include an evaluation of **environmental impact** , **system constraints** , and **practical deployment considerations** , such as scalability, feasibility, and stakeholder implications. The task will be conducted in groups; however, **demonstration, report, and oral examination will be assessed individually** to ensure ownership and depth of understanding. 

The Midterm Assessment would be on an open-ended _**Complex Engineering Problems (EP)**_ , which has multiple solutions. The students will **utilize IoT technology to monitor the energy consumption** of a computer lab or an IT infrastructure for justified periods. They will prepare an energy monitoring dashboard/webpage with detailed and summarized information (e.g., wattage at any minute, energy usage in kWh after each day, daily operating costs, energy usage trend charts, IT load vs. non-IT loads, for individual devices and/or collectively for the infrastructure as a whole). The dashboard may have the capability to control the electrical equipment. 

_The task should be performed in groups; however, report, demonstration and oral examination would be taken individually for each student on the Midterm Assessment week. Detailed instructions will be provided in class._

## **Academic Integrity:** 

Any form of cheating, plagiarism, personification, falsification of a document as well as any other form of dishonest behavior related to obtaining academic gain or the avoidance of evaluative exercises committed by a student is an academic offence under the Academic Code of Conduct and **may lead to severe penalties as decided by the Disciplinary Committee of the university. Special Instructions:** 

- Students are expected to attend all classes and examinations. A student MUST have at least 80% class attendance to sit for the final exam. 

- Students will not be allowed to enter the classroom after 20 minutes of the starting time. 

- For plagiarism, the grade will automatically become zero for that exam/assignment. 

- Normally there will be **NO make-up exam** . Proper supporting documents in favor of the reason of missing the final exam have to be presented with the application. **It is the responsibility of the student to arrange an Incomplete Exam within the deadline mentioned in the Academic Calendar in consultation with the Course Instructor** .
